[Windows 11]
Windows 11 is Microsoft's latest desktop operating system. Minimum requirements: 1GHz dual-core 64-bit CPU, 4GB RAM, 64GB storage, TPM 2.0, DirectX 12. Common issues: upgrade compatibility errors (run PC Health Check tool), Start menu freezes (restart Windows Explorer), Wi-Fi dropping (update network drivers via Device Manager). For activation issues, go to Settings > System > Activation.

[Microsoft 365]
Microsoft 365 (formerly Office 365) includes Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams, and OneDrive. Subscription tiers: Personal (1 user), Family (up to 6 users), Business Basic/Standard/Premium. Common issues: license not activating (sign out and back in at account.microsoft.com), Outlook not syncing (remove and re-add account), OneDrive sync errors (check storage quota at onedrive.live.com). Admin center: admin.microsoft.com.

[Microsoft Teams]
Microsoft Teams is a collaboration platform for chat, video calls, and file sharing. Common issues: camera/mic not working (check privacy settings in Windows > Camera/Microphone), meeting join errors (clear Teams cache: %appdata%\Microsoft\Teams), notifications not showing (Settings > Notifications > turn on Activity). Teams admin portal: admin.teams.microsoft.com. For enterprise deployments, contact your IT admin.

[Xbox]
Xbox services cover Xbox Series X/S, Xbox One, and Xbox Game Pass. Common issues: can't sign in (check Xbox Live status at status.xbox.com), game won't launch (clear local saved games, power cycle console), billing questions (visit account.microsoft.com > Subscriptions). Xbox Game Pass Ultimate includes console, PC, and cloud gaming. For hardware defects within warranty, initiate a Device Service Order at support.xbox.com.

[Azure]
Microsoft Azure is Microsoft's cloud computing platform. Common issues: subscription billing questions (visit portal.azure.com > Cost Management), service outages (check azure.status.microsoft.com), access/permissions errors (review IAM roles in the Azure portal). For enterprise support, use the Azure support plan portal. Developer docs: docs.microsoft.com/azure.

[Surface Devices]
Microsoft Surface lineup includes Surface Pro, Laptop, Book, Studio, and Go. Common issues: touchscreen not responding (calibrate via Control Panel > Tablet PC Settings), battery draining fast (check Background Apps in Settings > Privacy), driver issues (run Surface Diagnostic Toolkit from the Microsoft Store). Warranty and service: account.microsoft.com/devices. Out-of-warranty repairs can be arranged via an authorized service provider.

[Account and Billing]
Microsoft accounts (MSA) are used across all Microsoft services. Common issues: forgot password (reset at account.microsoft.com/password), suspicious activity (review sign-in activity and enable two-factor auth at account.microsoft.com/security), unauthorized charges (dispute at account.microsoft.com/billing). For child accounts and family safety, visit account.microsoft.com/family.

[Escalation Policy]
Escalate to a human agent when: (1) the customer expresses frustration or anger repeatedly, (2) the issue involves account compromise or unauthorized financial transactions, (3) the customer has already tried the suggested steps and the issue persists, (4) the query is outside the scope of standard product support (e.g., legal, accessibility accommodations, media inquiries). To escalate, direct the customer to contact Microsoft Support at support.microsoft.com or call 1-800-642-7676.
